Blogger import

In Blogger, open Settings, scroll to Manage blog, choose Back up content, then select Download to obtain your blog's export (an .atom or .xml file).

The importer turns this file into an archive (.zip) containing Markdown files. It preserves published posts and pages, their original URLs and publication dates, and labels, and downloads images and linked PDF files alongside the Markdown.


If set, links to this site inside posts are rewritten as root-relative paths (e.g. /2025/07/post.html).
